Why off-plan buyers hesitate, and what 1:1 scale changes

A floor plan is a promise. A twin is a place you can stand in.

22 October 2026

Buying off-plan means committing to a space nobody has walked through yet. That gap between drawing and reality is where hesitation lives.

Plans, renders and brochures all ask the buyer to do the same work: translate lines and pictures into a felt sense of room size, ceiling height and light. Some people are good at it. Many aren’t, and they wait until the building exists.

Scale is the missing piece

At true 1:1 scale, a buyer doesn’t estimate the kitchen. They stand in it, turn around and see whether the island leaves room to move. The same twin carries site context, so the view from the balcony is the actual view.

Don’t ask them to imagine. Let them step inside the unbuild.
